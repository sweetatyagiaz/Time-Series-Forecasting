# Decomposition-Based Forecasting Models

## Overview

Decomposition models separate a time series into:

- Trend
- Seasonality
- Residuals

These models are widely used in business forecasting.

---

## Implemented Models

### Prophet

Developed by Meta.

Features:

- Trend modeling
- Multiple seasonality
- Holiday effects
- Missing value handling

---

### STL Forecast

Seasonal Trend decomposition using LOESS.

Components:

- Trend
- Seasonal
- Residual

---

### Seasonal Decompose

Classical decomposition approach.

---

## Best Use Cases

- Revenue forecasting
- Website traffic
- Demand planning
- KPI forecasting

---

## Advantages

- Highly interpretable
- Handles seasonality well

## Limitations

- Less effective for highly nonlinear datasets
