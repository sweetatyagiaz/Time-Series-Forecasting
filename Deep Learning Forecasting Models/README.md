# Deep Learning Forecasting Models

## Overview

Deep learning models automatically learn temporal patterns directly from data.

Ideal for large datasets and complex forecasting tasks.

---

## Implemented Models

### LSTM

Long Short-Term Memory.

Captures long-range dependencies.

---

### GRU

Gated Recurrent Unit.

Faster alternative to LSTM.

---

### TCN

Temporal Convolutional Network.

Parallelizable and efficient.

---

### N-BEATS

State-of-the-art deep forecasting architecture.

---

## Best Use Cases

- Stock forecasting
- Energy forecasting
- Demand forecasting
- Sensor data forecasting

---

## Advantages

- Learns complex temporal patterns
- Minimal manual feature engineering

## Limitations

- Requires large datasets
- Higher computational cost
