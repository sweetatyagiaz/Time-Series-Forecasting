# Exponential Smoothing Models

## Overview

Exponential smoothing models assign greater importance to recent observations.

Useful when recent behavior is more relevant than older data.

---

## Implemented Models

### Simple Exponential Smoothing (SES)

No trend.
No seasonality.

---

### Holt's Linear Trend

Supports trend.

No seasonality.

---

### Holt-Winters

Supports:

- Trend
- Seasonality

Additive and Multiplicative variants available.

---

## Best Use Cases

- Retail sales
- Demand forecasting
- Inventory planning
- Energy consumption

---

## Advantages

- Simple
- Fast
- Good short-term accuracy

## Limitations

- Limited handling of nonlinear relationships
